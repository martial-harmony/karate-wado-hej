A kata (型) is a fixed sequence of techniques practised alone against imaginary opponents. Wadō-ryū has 15 traditional kata: the five Pinan kata and ten others. The meanings below are the commonly given ones; interpretations differ between sources.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| First level (1) | Första nivån (1) | Shodan | 初段 | 初段 |
| Second level (2) | Andra nivån (2) | Nidan | 二段 | 二段 |
| Third level (3) | Tredje nivån (3) | Sandan | 三段 | 三段 |
| Fourth level (4) | Fjärde nivån (4) | Yondan | 四段 | 四段 |
| Fifth level (5) | Femte nivån (5) | Godan | 五段 | 五段 |

## Notes – 使用说明

- Spellings vary between sources and organisations (for example Kushanku / Kūshankū, Seishan / Seisan, Naihanchi / Naifanchin).
- The order in which kata are taught, and which kata are required for each grade, depends on your dojo and association. Follow your instructor.
- Terms often used when studying kata, such as Bunkai (分解) and Embusen (演武線), are on the Wadō-ryū, Kata and Mindset page.
